Economic Modeling Club

About us

EMC is a student-led club at U-High for students interested in economics, data, and research.

What we do

We use economic models to explore how people make decisions and how economies change. Members work through ideas together, discuss their assumptions, and use data to examine them.

Our meetings

A meeting may begin with a short student-led lesson, followed by discussion or an activity in Google Sheets. Members practice building formulas, comparing results, and presenting what they find.

You do not need previous economics or spreadsheet experience to participate.

Our next steps

We are developing our first research paper and looking for a collaborator to help guide the work. We are also inviting guest speakers and preparing for our January 13, 2027 visit to the Federal Reserve Bank of Chicago.
