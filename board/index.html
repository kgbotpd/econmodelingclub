Current leadership

The students organizing EMC’s meetings, projects, and events.

2026–2027

Alexander Reyes

Co-founder

Alex Reyes, Co-Founder, is a junior at the University of Chicago Laboratory Schools, where he is an active member of the Finance Club and contributes to its student-run portfolio. He also serves on the board of B.R.A.V.E. Alex takes Principles of Microeconomics at the University of Chicago and is interested in how companies make investment decisions and why some communities attract more investment than others.

Outside of school, he is a member of the Junior Economic Club of Chicago, where he participates in initiatives focused on macroeconomic modeling and financial literacy. He works as an A-rank caddie at LakeShore Country Club and enjoys playing golf with friends in his free time.

Ben Lurye

Co-founder

Benjamin Lurye is a member of his school's Finance Club and co-founder of the Economic Modeling Club at U-High. He is passionate about economics and finance and has taken courses at the University of Chicago and Johns Hopkins University.

Outside of school, Ben serves on the board of the Junior Economic Club of Chicago as Director of Operations and Media. He has previously interned with companies such as Kargo and ThetaIX. He competes on a Chicago-based varsity crew team and has raced at the national level. He loves traveling and has a passion for documenting his travels through photography. In his free time, Ben is an avid cinephile who loves watching films, cheering on the Chicago Bears, and spending time rowing on the Chicago River.

Nikolas Gulyayev

Director of Technology

Nikolas Gulyayev is currently a junior at the University of Chicago Laboratory Schools. He is the Director of Technology, using his knowledge of computer science to support members of the Economics Modeling Club with website building, model visualization, and other technology driven aids. In his free time, Nikolas enjoys solving Math Problems, and he is a member of the Math Team as well as the Computer Science Team. Nikolas also enjoys playing tennis, representing Lab School in the first singles spot last year.

Ian Galvez Garcia

Director of Opportunity & Outreach
